CALUNAH (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use www.calunah.org, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Contact & membership forms: your name, email address, phone number, graduation year, chapter/location, and any message you send.
  • Newsletter sign-up: your name and email address.
  • Donations & tickets: processed securely by our payment partners (Stripe, PayPal, Donorbox, Zelle). We never see or store your full card or bank details.
  • Usage data: basic, anonymous analytics (pages visited, general location, device type) to help us improve the site.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and process membership applications.
  • To send newsletters and updates you subscribed to.
  • To process donations and event ticket purchases and send receipts.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

3. Payment Processing

All payments are handled by trusted third-party processors — Stripe, PayPal, Donorbox, and Zelle — each PCI-DSS compliant. Your payment card information is entered on their secure systems and is never stored on our website.

4. How We Protect Your Information

Our website is served entirely over encrypted HTTPS. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Access to submitted information is limited to authorized CALUNAH administrators.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to count visits and understand which content is helpful. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site links to external services (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, payment pages). Their use of your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Your Choices

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter anytime via the link in any email, or by emailing us.
  • Request access or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the latest version.
