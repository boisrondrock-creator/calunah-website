Message from Our President

For over three decades, CALUNAH has stood as a bridge — connecting UNAH graduates across the Haitian diaspora, channeling resources back to the campus and communities that shaped us, and honoring the Adventist values of faith, education, and service that unite us all.

This year, we continued to award scholarships to deserving students at l'Université Adventiste d'Haïti, expanded our mentorship network across chapters, raised funds through our annual Saturday Giving program and High Tea Fundraiser, and delivered community outreach programs on multiple continents. Every dollar raised, every mentor matched, every event organized — reflects the dedication of our alumni family worldwide.

To our donors, grantors, partners, and members: thank you. CALUNAH's impact is your impact. Together, we are building a stronger Haiti, one graduate at a time.

Program Highlights
Six pillars of impact — active every year since 1993

Scholarship Program

Our flagship program provides annual scholarships to outstanding UNAH students demonstrating academic excellence and financial need. Since 1993, we have awarded more than $250,000 in scholarships, transforming the lives of hundreds of Haitian students.

$250,000+ awarded to date
Multiple scholars per year

Mentorship Network

CALUNAH alumni in medicine, law, engineering, business, and education volunteer as mentors for UNAH students. Our network spans 20+ countries, giving students access to guidance they could never find locally.

500+ students mentored
Mentors in 20+ countries

Campus Development

CALUNAH funds infrastructure and academic resources at UNAH's Diquini campus — from library books and lab equipment to facility upgrades. Every physical improvement we fund serves thousands of students for generations.

30+ projects completed
Library & lab investments

Community Service

Rooted in Adventist values of compassion, our chapters organize annual outreach events in Haiti and diaspora cities including health fairs, food drives, back-to-school drives, and disaster relief after major earthquakes.

Annual events in Haiti & US
Disaster relief partnerships

Fundraising Programs

Our annual calendar includes the High Tea Fundraiser, Saturday Giving broadcasts, Gala events, and online campaigns — each designed to fund scholarships and campus projects while celebrating the CALUNAH community.

12+ annual events
Broadcast & virtual events

Cultural Preservation

Through Week of Prayer, cultural programs, our e-newsletter, and alumni spotlights, CALUNAH preserves the Haitian Adventist heritage and keeps diaspora members connected to their UNAH roots.

32 Years of Milestones
Key moments in CALUNAH's history of service
1993
CALUNAH Founded
Pastor Roberne J. and Mrs. Lynouse Dubreuze Saint-Louis founded CALUNAH to unite UNAH graduates across the diaspora.
Early 2000s
First Scholarships Awarded
CALUNAH officially launched its scholarship program, awarding its first grants to deserving UNAH students.
2010
Haiti Earthquake Relief
CALUNAH mobilized disaster relief for Haiti following the devastating 2010 earthquake, coordinating donations and aid to UNAH's campus community.
2016
IRS 501(c)(3) Status Achieved
CALUNAH earned federal tax-exempt status, enabling tax-deductible donations and eligibility for foundation grants.
2020
Virtual Programs Launched
The pandemic accelerated CALUNAH's digital reach. Saturday Giving broadcasts and virtual Week of Prayer events connected alumni globally.
2024–2025
$250K+ in Scholarships Milestone
CALUNAH surpassed $250,000 in cumulative scholarship awards, with an expanded donor base across six chapters and 20+ countries.
